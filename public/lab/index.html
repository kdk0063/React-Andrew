Three ways to open the studio

Each concept uses the real covers and copy, with one creative feature of its own. Try them on desktop and on a phone.

  1. AContentsThe landing page as a book’s table of contents, in big type. Hover a chapter and its cover follows your cursor, tilting as it moves, while the ADK needle swings east.
  2. BEast, v2Developed: the books turn in 3D as they pass the needle, a seal-red sun rises from 2017 to 2026, a true-to-scale career ruler shows each chapter’s real span (click a bar to jump), the year rolls like an odometer, and arrows or ← → step through chapters.
  3. B1East, v1The first pass, kept for comparison.
  4. COpen the bookOne hardcover with the new ADK STUDIO wordmark. Scroll and the cover swings open: your intro on the left page, the chapters on the right.
  5. DThe libraryThe Nov 1 mockup, developed: words rise in, four covers are dealt onto the page and tilt toward your cursor, real spines at their true sizes pull off the shelf into the Projects page, and the side-project posters keep their real art.
  6. —The Nov 1 mockupFor comparison: the earlier one-page mockup with four covers in the hero.
