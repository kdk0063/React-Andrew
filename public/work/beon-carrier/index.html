Beon Carrier

NTG's free load board for trucking carriers. Instead of phone calls, spreadsheets and several windows open at once, a carrier sees available loads, the rate and the route on one screen, and bids on or books a load in a few clicks. I was a full-stack developer on it for almost five years.

01 — Load board

Find the right load

Every available load on one page, filtered by origin, radius and equipment. Opening a load shows the stops, weight, equipment and rate next to the route on a map. Carriers can save lane preferences to see matching loads first, and the app recommends loads too.

02 — Bid and book

Book now, or bid

Instant bid-and-book: a carrier can book a load right away at NTG's rate, or place up to three bids on it. Each bid is shown as approved, under review, rejected or cancelled, and a rejected bid still offers the load at the Beon rate, so the carrier doesn't lose it.

03 — After the haul

Paperwork and faster pay

Booked loads keep their details, status and a downloadable rate confirmation. Carriers upload proof of delivery in the app instead of fielding check calls, and Fast Pay (1-day or 2-day) pays them soon after the job is done.
