A CRM built for one business

Off-the-shelf CRMs are built for sales teams. A showroom runs on something else: measurements, designs, deposits, supplier orders, deliveries and installs. So I built the admin tool around exactly that flow. Every job is a card that moves from first call to final install, and the follow-ups, orders and money stay attached to it. Product
01 — Pipeline

Every job, at a glance

Jobs move through lanes that match the shop: lead and measure, design and quote, paid and ordered, assemble and deliver, install, complete. Each card shows the customer, value, contractor, supplier and the next thing that needs doing. The board stays in sync with the Trello board the team already used, so nobody had to switch tools overnight.

02 — Follow-ups

Nothing falls through the cracks

Everything that is due sits in one list: overdue, today, this week. Simple rules do the chasing. A quote with no reply after seven days creates a follow-up and drafts the email; a ticked payment moves the card and asks for the purchase order; a finished job sends the review request.

03 — Orders

What is ordered, and when it lands

Cabinet and countertop orders across every supplier, with PO numbers, status, progress and ETA. Late shipments are flagged before the customer has to ask.

04 — Partners

Contractors and suppliers as real records

Contractors and fabricators get their own records: jobs this year, open work, revenue, win rate, terms and what they owe, with every linked job one click away.

05 — Insights

Where the business really comes from

Which lead sources turn into completed jobs, how many inquiries make it to install, revenue by contractor, and where jobs wait longest, so the owner knows what to fix next.
