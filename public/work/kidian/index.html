01 — The problem

Party planning is a long to-do list

For parents of young children, a birthday means juggling a venue, entertainment, catering, a cake, decorations and invitations. Each one is a separate search, phone call and booking. Kidian puts them all in one app: browse and compare vendors with photos, video and reviews, then reserve. Think OpenTable, but for kids' parties.

02 — The app

From search to a booked party

Search by city, date and number of kids, or narrow by category: birthday, clown, party room. Vendor pages show ratings, age range and bookable packages with prices and set-up space. Promotions and Kidian credit apply at checkout, and add-ons like a custom cake can be sorted by price, distance or rating.

The booking flow was benchmarked against hotel and restaurant reservation apps, and the add-ons against e-commerce carts.
