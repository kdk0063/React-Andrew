Genesis Massage

A calm, booking-first website for a one-therapist massage practice in Vienna, Virginia. A client picks a session, sees the real open times and books in under a minute, and the owner runs the whole schedule from his own Google Calendar, with no paid backend. I ran the project and designed the system, working with Christina Park on the front end.

The brief CALM · CLEAR · BOOKABLE
Goal

Every page leads to a booking

Present the two sessions clearly, earn trust with a calm brand and real Google reviews, and put “Book” within one tap from anywhere on the site.

Constraint

No backend bill to pay every month

A small practice shouldn’t pay for servers or a booking SaaS. The system had to run on tools the owner already has.

Ownership

The owner stays in control

Hours, prices and blocked-off days change without a developer. The owner edits his calendar or a spreadsheet, and the site follows.

Trust

No double bookings, no spam

Two people choosing the same slot at the same moment can’t both get it, and bots can’t fill the calendar with fake appointments.

System GOOGLE AS THE BACKEND
Architecture

A fast static site, with Google doing the heavy lifting

The React site is static and deployed to Netlify from GitHub. Booking calls a Google Apps Script web app, which reads live availability from the owner’s Google Calendar, takes hours and prices from a Google Sheet, writes the appointment back to the calendar and emails both sides. The owner’s calendar is the single source of truth, so there is no database to keep in sync.

Decision

Apps Script instead of a server

It runs on Google’s infrastructure at no cost, already has access to the owner’s Calendar, Sheets and Gmail, and has nothing to patch or scale.

Decision

The calendar is the database

Open times are calculated from the owner’s real calendar, so a personal appointment he adds on his phone blocks that slot on the site straight away.

Safeguard

Lock, then check again

Each booking takes a script lock and re-checks the slot before writing the event, so two clients can never get the same time. Open times are cached for a short while to keep the calendar fast.

Safeguard

Bots stay out

Cloudflare Turnstile checks every booking in the background, with no puzzle to solve, and the script verifies the token before it touches the calendar.

Booking flow SESSION · TIME · DETAILS · DONE
01 — Session

Pick a session

Two options and nothing more: a 60-minute focused reset or a 100-minute full session, with the price shown up front. Every “Book” button on the site opens this page with its session already selected.

02 — Time

See only times that are really free

Choosing a day asks the script for that day’s open slots, worked out from the owner’s calendar, opening hours and the session length. Past days are disabled, and times are shown in Eastern time.

03 — Details

Four fields and a note

Name, email and phone, plus room for special requests. There’s nothing to pay online: payment is taken after the session, and changes are made with a phone call, which is how the owner prefers to work.

04 — Confirmed

Booked, on both calendars

On “Confirm Booking” the script verifies the bot check, takes the lock, makes sure the slot is still free and creates the event on the owner’s calendar. The client and the owner each get a confirmation email within seconds.
