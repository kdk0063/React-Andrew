08 · Next

To decide

Once a contender is picked, these are the next steps.

1. Pick the contender

Or combine them, for example the Shooting Star A as the mark with the Brush ADK as the merch wordmark.

2. Hand-tune the vector

Fix the AI wobble: straighten the star points, even out the stroke weights, and make a simplified small version.

3. Put it on the site

Header logo, favicon, the share image and the 404 page, then the case-study pages.

4. Print a test

Business cards and one tee. Brush texture and thin tails look different on paper and fabric than on screen.
