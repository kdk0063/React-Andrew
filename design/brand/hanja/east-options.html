東 — new drawings

The current 東 has no frame around 日 and its middle stroke stops above the legs, so it reads more like 申 or 車. Every option below follows the real structure: 一 on top, a full 日 box with its middle line, the centre stroke running through to the bottom, and both legs springing from under the box. 奎 stays as it is.
