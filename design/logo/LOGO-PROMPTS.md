# Nano Banana: ADK logo directions

The idea: Andrew Dong Kyu Kim. 東奎 means "east star", so the star always
sits on the RIGHT side of the mark (east), never the left.

## How to run it

- Upload 1–2 of your style references with each prompt and say "use only
  as a style reference, do not copy". Don't ask it to recreate someone
  else's logo.
- Ask for 4 variations each time, then iterate on the best one:
  "keep this exactly, but make the star sharper / thinner / more to the right".
- When you have a favourite, send it back: it gets redrawn as a clean
  vector (SVG) so it is sharp at every size and fully yours.

Paste this ending onto every prompt:

> Flat logo artwork only: solid black on a plain off-white background,
> no gradients, no texture, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text.
> Centered, square image, generous margin. Show 4 variations.

---

## 1. Calligraphic A with an east star

> A single capital letter "A" logo in a sharp, calligraphic blackletter
> style with flared, brush-like strokes and tapered ends. A four-point
> sparkle star with concave, needle-sharp points is fused into the RIGHT
> end of the A's crossbar, so the crossbar shoots out to the right and
> becomes the star, as if the letter points east. The star is part of the
> same black shape, not a separate icon.

## 2. Negative-space A with a star inside

> A bold, geometric capital "A" logo built from smooth, curved shapes,
> where a four-point sparkle star is carved out of the letter in negative
> space on the RIGHT side of the A, where the crossbar meets the right leg.
> Elegant, minimal, high contrast. White on solid black.

(For this one, swap the ending to "solid white on black".)

## 3. Signature monogram

> A handwritten signature monogram "adk" written in one fast, confident
> pen stroke, slanting upward to the right, with the final stroke of the
> k ending in a small four-point sparkle star on the right. Below it, the
> words "ANDREW DONG KYU KIM" in small, widely spaced geometric sans-serif
> capitals, with the signature crossing through the line of type.

## 4. Graffiti tag

> A graffiti hand-style tag of the letters "ADK", fast and confident,
> like a b-boy's marker tag, with sharp flicks and one long underline
> stroke that shoots out to the right and ends in a small four-point
> sparkle star. Clean, readable, no drips, no wall.

## 5. Brush calligraphy 奎 (star)

> The Chinese character 奎 (meaning "star") written in expressive East
> Asian brush calligraphy, white, inside a tall black rectangle, with one
> horizontal brush stroke extending outside the rectangle to the RIGHT and
> ending in a sharp point. Small spaced capitals "ADK STUDIO" set
> vertically along the left edge of the rectangle.

(Brush characters are where these tools most often get the strokes
wrong. Check that the character still reads as 奎.)
